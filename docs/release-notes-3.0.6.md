# Orgtree 3.0.6

- **Circle view gathers sparse rings.** In the circle view, a ring with only
  a few agents no longer spreads them around the whole circle. They sit side
  by side in one arc centred at the bottom, and the arc grows in both
  directions as agents join until its ends meet at the top. After that the
  ring grows in radius, as before.
- **Effort chip only on the canvas card.** The thinking-effort chip no
  longer appears in the header of an agent's full desk. It still shows on
  the agent's card on the zoomed canvas, and you still change effort from the
  composer.

This update contains only these two changes on top of 3.0.5.
