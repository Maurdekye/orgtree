# Orgtree 4.7.1

- **Agent links.** Clicking an agent's name opens their chat only inside Chats. Elsewhere, it focuses their desk on the canvas or switches to their desk in Attention. You can still right-click and choose **Open > Chat** from anywhere.
- **Replies in Chats.** Replies to presentations and tickets show a label, the original quote, and your message in one bubble.
- **Canvas camera.** Turn off **Fit the view around pinned windows** in **Settings > Display > Desk** to fit and focus using the whole canvas. The setting is on by default.
