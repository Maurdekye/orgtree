# Orgtree 4.7.2

- **Message menus.** Right-click a message or mail link to jump to its copy in an inbox, outbox, chat, or transcript. An agent’s Sent includes mail it sent to outside parties.
- **Waiting mail.** A project’s **Mail > Listeners** tab shows queued mail and separates **Mail waited** from **Agent waited**.
- **Mail hubs.** Saving other hubs keeps this computer’s hub in the list.
- **Desk header.** Inherited access shows one badge per project. Click a badge to open that project.
