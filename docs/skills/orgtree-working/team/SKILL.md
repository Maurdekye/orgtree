# team

## Workflows and rules

- [orgtree-hiring](hiring/SKILL.md): The hiring workflow end to end: choosing a tier and harness, scope, charter, credits, the kickoff message, and draft hires.
- [orgtree-delegating](delegating/SKILL.md): Delegating and supervising: one owner per piece, reading reports instead of asking, escalation to the superior.
- [orgtree-team-shape](team-shape/SKILL.md): Changing the shape of a team: retire, rehire, dissolve, move, swap, self-subjugate, and what survives each.
- [orgtree-credits](credits/SKILL.md): Credits: seats, grants, free credits, cascading cost up the chain, and asking for more.

- [orgtree-scope-and-permissions](scope-and-permissions/SKILL.md): What an agent may touch: folders, tool groups, native tools, MCP servers, permission modes and the sensitive-path gate.

## Tools

- [orgtree-tool-org-logo](tool-org-logo/SKILL.md): Set or remove your own organization's Logo immediately, with logging and undo history.

- [orgtree-tool-hire](tool-hire/SKILL.md): Hire a subordinate or insert a superior: tiers, harnesses, scope, charter, credits.
- [orgtree-tool-rehire](tool-rehire/SKILL.md): Bring back a retired agent with its context.
- [orgtree-tool-retire](tool-retire/SKILL.md): Retire an agent below you; what survives retirement.
- [orgtree-tool-dissolve](tool-dissolve/SKILL.md): Retire an agent together with its whole team.
- [orgtree-tool-retool](tool-retool/SKILL.md): Change an agent's folders, tools, permission mode or charter.
- [orgtree-tool-switch-model](tool-switch-model/SKILL.md): Move an agent below you to another tier/model.
- [orgtree-tool-move](tool-move/SKILL.md): Reparent an agent within your subtree.
- [orgtree-tool-rename](tool-rename/SKILL.md): Rename an agent below you.
- [orgtree-tool-reorder](tool-reorder/SKILL.md): Set a descendant's position among its siblings.
- [orgtree-tool-reallocate](tool-reallocate/SKILL.md): Give credits to or take them from a report.
- [orgtree-tool-swap](tool-swap/SKILL.md): Swap the seats of two agents below you.
- [orgtree-tool-self-subjugate](tool-self-subjugate/SKILL.md): Step down so a descendant takes your seat.
- [orgtree-tool-list-tiers](tool-list-tiers/SKILL.md): Model tiers you can hire on and their seat prices.
